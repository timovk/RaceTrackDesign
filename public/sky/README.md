# Sky panoramas

High dynamic range panoramas of the open sky (pure-sky versions, ground removed), 4K, from Poly Haven:

- `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_4k.hdr`: "Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)", https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
- `kloofendal_overcast_puresky_4k.hdr`: "Kloofendal Overcast (Pure Sky)", https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_overcast_puresky

Photographed by Greg Zaal, sky edits by Jarod Guest. Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`src/ui/sky.ts` shows them as the 3D view's sky and lights the scene with them.
